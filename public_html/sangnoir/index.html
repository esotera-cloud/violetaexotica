Charles Sangnoir is a musician and producer from Portugal, currently living in Paris.

He has written music for television, radio and cinema, and has played more than 500 concerts across cities like Lisbon, Oporto, Paris, Montpellier, London, Madrid or Vigo.

As a producer and/or session musician, he has participated the making of over 50 records.

He has featured as host pianist in 2 Portuguese tv shows over 300+ episodes, and dedicates himself as well to painting, gastronomy and the esoteric sciences.
